- S-only EKF: `H_s=[s_x,s_y,s_ψ,0]`; Joint EKF: one prior-linearized `H=[H_r;H_s]`; SR4: one Range EKF posterior then re-evaluate `s,H_s` at new position and apply **one** scalar s update; MAP4: minimize original fixed prior+Range+s joint J, range-first warm start only. SR4≠MAP4.
- Bias observation: `H_{s,b}=0` but predicted `P_{pose,b}≠0` from `F_{ψb}=−Δt`, so `K_b=P_{b,pose}H_s^T/S_s` can be nonzero. No new ground-truth heading or bias is delivered directly by LP power ratio alone.

Single seed and positive bias signs only; no generalization.
- An anchor-centered perfect circle has `H_r t=0` for the instantaneous tangential unit vector t. Range first primarily improves radial uncertainty; its effect on Heading is through geometry and cross-covariance, not a guarantee that circular travel favors SR4.

## 4. New independent Yaw Sweep Heading factor proposal (NOT IMPLEMENTED)

Goal: every 10s / 20s or conditional NIS/heading uncertainty event, pause at the next suitable station or safe site, rotate body around center by **−10° to +10° relative to current estimated heading** (test 2° steps = 11 headings), collect repeated `s_j, P1_j, P2_j, first path tap, range_j, optional quality` with accurately timestamped **relative yaw increments** (wheel/gyro). Change to heading posterior is a **pseudo-observation** with uncertainty, NOT physical ground truth. Real GT only independent encoder/LiDAR/mocap/offline truth.
- Stationary scan measurement model: `s_j=h_s(p,ψ_0+δ_j)+m_s(δ_j)+ε_j`. `δ_j` controlled/measured but gyro bias and rotation-center drift uncertain. `m_s(δ_j)` represents multipath polarization response not modeled by frozen LoS LUT.
- Start with two competing models: (A) correlated noise, no multipath; (B) within-±10° smooth unknown common offset `m_0` plus correlated residuals. Fitting `ψ_0,m_0` jointly can suppress a yaw-independent multipath offset but is **not identifiable** when `∂s/∂ψ` is nearly constant over scan: a ψ shift looks like another offset.
- (I_{psi|m_0}=g^T(C^{-1}−C^{-1}1(1^TC^{-1}1)^{-1}1^TC^{-1})g), (g_j=∂_ψ h_s(p,ψ_0+δ_j)). Use this conditional information to choose informative yaw samples and reject uninformative scans. Unknown `x,y,b` must be included as nuisance or integrated via prior, not held at truth.
- A robust full scan `p(oldsymbol s|mathbf x,delta_{1:K})` can be added ONCE as a dedicated factor; **do not update EKF with each scan RF sample and then also add pseudo-Heading from the same samples**. If posterior heading is multimodal, keep mixture/gated hypotheses rather than DR-root-pick and artificially tiny Gaussian R.
- Experiment controls: original no-scan SR4; same K repeated packets at fixed yaw; physical yaw sweep ±10° (also test ±5°, ±20° as preregistered sensitivity); LoS, Full; mount0/45; B0/L0/L1/L2; 10s/20s cadence; frozen source negative/positive control; no hidden truth and no retrospective selection. Track heading/position/bias RMSE, NEES, 95% coverage, false confidently incorrect pseudo-heading, information gain and extra stop/rotation time.
- **Critical data dependency**: existing 14 original frozen trajectories have exactly one RF orientation per pose. A physically valid FULL multipath yaw sweep requires additional orientation-specific H(CIR) from FFD full-wave/multipath simulator at *fixed position with rotated RX antenna*, or real repeated orientation measurements. Sweeping only `φ_rx` inside LoS LUT generates hypothetical **LoS reference**, not actual Full RF measurements; must not be reported as Full sweep success.
- **Implementation separation**: proposed `yaw_sweep_capture.py` (capture, quality, timestamps), `yaw_sweep_likelihood.py` (multi-angle robust likelihood), `yaw_sweep_factor.py` (one factor/pseudo-measurement with covariance and source IDs), `yaw_sweep_audit.py` (source/duplicated evidence/clock/motion/GT isolation), `yaw_sweep_eval.py` (offline only). No change to current P3 adapter, SR4 baseline, or R0 frozen RF model in the first comparison. Scan module must output HOLD/NO_INFORMATION rather than fabricated GT if information weak.
- Physical references: prior UWB angle-dependent antenna transfer-function methods and polarization measurement of multipath exist (e.g. [UWB transfer function AoA, Sensors 2019](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6832923/); Chang/Scholtz MILCOM 2004 DOI 10.1109/MILCOM.2004.1493268). Neither proves this specific ±10° dual LP stationary scan method, which is a testable proposal.
