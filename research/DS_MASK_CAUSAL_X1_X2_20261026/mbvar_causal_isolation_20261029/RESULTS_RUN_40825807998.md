## Matched-prior one-step

For R5-A in the 60-120 s window, changing only the same-prior one-step R multiplier 1 -> 8 gives Heading RMSE 7.584 -> 7.607 deg and XY RMSE 77.503 -> 77.664 cm. The large benefits of recursive full-R scaling are therefore NOT explained by an immediate one-step improvement alone. The prior-feedback and repeated updates matter.

## Conclusions and boundaries

1. Temporal residual arrangement affects recurring filter accuracy, but the permutation also changes fine-scale conditional geometry alignment. The causal effect of autocorrelation alone remains unisolated.
2. Changing observation weight at fixed input/mask strongly changes long-horizon trajectory accuracy. This is an intervention on gain and covariance, but not evidence of the specific physical cause of overconfidence.
3. R5-B is a counterexample to blanket residual decorrelation: destroying temporal alignment significantly worsened it.
4. Need next: controlled surrogate preserving fine-grained conditional residual statistics; multiple seed/trajectory environments; cross-validation of weighting model; self-gate separate study.
