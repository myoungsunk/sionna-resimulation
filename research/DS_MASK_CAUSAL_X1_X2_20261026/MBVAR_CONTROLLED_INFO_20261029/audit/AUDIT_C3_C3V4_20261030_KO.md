## 2. Three different root mechanisms, not one convergence failure

### F1 — spatial covariance-factor realization is not portable under same random seed (demonstrated)

`IC.psd_sqrt(K)` uses `w,V=np.linalg.eigh(K)`, `Lhalf=V*sqrt(w)[None,:]` (non-symmetric factor).
For \(\Sigma=V\Lambda V^\top\), \(L=V\Lambda^{1/2}\) satisfies \(LL^\top=\Sigma\), but each eigenvector may change sign; nearly degenerate eigenspaces can also rotate between LAPACK implementations. With fixed \(\xi\), the random draw \(L\xi\) can therefore change **even while the theoretical covariance law is the same**. The near-singular 180×180 world-pose kernel reported in the local diagnostic has min/max eigenvalues ~\(-1.51\times10^{-14}\), \(31.197\); output gaps up to \(10^{-8}\). That supports this mechanism. No evidence of scientific leakage from this difference; it violates frozen per-field realization parity.

**Fix:** freeze and hash one concrete factor `Lhalf` **and the generated observation/input arrays**; distinguish `GENERATOR_DISTRIBUTION` (target covariance/PSD) from `INPUT_EXACT` (exact selected realization). Checking only trace/norm/\(\Sigma r\) is not collision-free and is not a substitute for full canonical SHA256 of all float64 \(\Sigma\) bytes.

### F2 — narrow FFD-kink numerical ambiguity remains (demonstrated in C3, not repeated in C3V4)

`field=10, LOW, TIME_1, sensor=2, ORACLE_CORR_BATCH`: \(|\Delta z|=4.44\times10^{-15}\) but state replay delta 0.0764 d_ref (position-equivalent 1.53 mm **only if x/y is the maximal component**; the CSV does not store the component-wise alternative-state difference), cost delta 0.0006698 chi², diagonal C10-P delta 0.2486%. Old E' stops at cost descent threshold 1e-3 chi². Local developer-only three-perturbation test with `polish_tol=1e-9` / cap 300 reduced largest state spread to 0.00187 d_ref and cost spread to 6.53e-7 chi², but **did not reach the original 1e-6 d_ref** bound. These are diagnostics, not fresh independent proof of a universal cure. Saving `Lhalf` alone does not eliminate architecture-dependent FFD evaluation/branch sensitivity at the knot.

### F3 — near-equivalent competing minima/weak identification, NOT input reproducibility

Same field in BOTH C3 versions:
`field=11, REV60, TIME_HIGH_5, sensor=0, ORACLE_CORR_BATCH`.
The `psi+2` candidate is 0.0511 chi² lower and 2.21068 d_ref (position-equivalent 4.42 cm **only if x/y dominates**; actual component-wise alternate-state difference must be independently reconstructed) away from E'; reported `E_vs_best_sdC10≈0.5131`. C3v4 replay `d_z=8.88e-15`, `d_state_dref` ~0, so this is NOT failure to reproduce a fixed estimate. It is failure of `STATE_STABILITY_FIXED` against an alternate start. This may be a flat/sloppy direction or a separate nearby basin; `C3 C_star` is *minimum among a fixed start grid*, not a global optimum. Deeper polish around E' does not guarantee jumping to this solution; in S1V4 it took 0 steps.

Do **not** call F3 merely a "numerical precision bug" or erase it by a relaxed replay tolerance. A genuine state-identifiability limitation matters to reported P, Heading and H2. A multiple-start or profile-likelihood diagnostic can resolve whether a point-MAP reference is adequate.
